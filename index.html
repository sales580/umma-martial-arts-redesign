    Questions

    Quick answers.

    Who can join UMMA?

    Anyone aged 5 and over. There are kids’ classes, men’s and boys’ classes, sisters’ classes, and family and all-ages classes. The timetable shows who each class is for.

    How much does it cost?

    Membership is $80 a month, or $800 a year, which saves $160. New students also pay a one-time registration of $120, which includes a uniform. A family discount is available, so ask us. You can cancel any time.

    Can I train at other centres?

    Yes. Your membership works at every UMMA centre at no extra cost.

    Are classes separate for brothers and sisters?

    At selected centres, yes. Salaheddin runs separate sisters’ and brothers’ classes on Sundays, and Darul-Hijra has a men’s class on Saturdays. If you are looking for a women-only class, message us and we will point you to the right one.

    Is it safe for children?

    Students wear protective gear when sparring, and instructors guide every class. Parents are asked to pick children up promptly when class ends.
